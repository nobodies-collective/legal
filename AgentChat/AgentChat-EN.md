# AI Assistant Terms

**Asociación Nobodies Collective**

---

\*In the event of any discrepancy between the Spanish version and the English version, the Spanish version shall prevail.

---

## 1. Introduction

These Terms ("Terms") govern the use of the conversational assistant integrated into the Asociación Nobodies Collective application ("the Association"). By activating the assistant, the Volunteer accepts the terms set out below.

The assistant is an optional tool. The Volunteer can use the application in full without activating it.

## 2. Definitions

- **Association** — Asociación Nobodies Collective.
- **Volunteer** — The authenticated person who uses the assistant.
- **Assistant** — The conversational component embedded in the application, which answers questions about the application itself, the Association's processes, and information the Volunteer has access to.
- **AI Provider** — The third-party provider that hosts the language model to which the assistant sends queries to generate responses.

## 3. Nature of the Service

### 3.1 How it works

Each message sent to the assistant — together with a minimal context about the Volunteer (display name, language, active teams, current roles) and the page or section from which it is invoked — is transmitted to the AI Provider, which returns an automatically generated response.

The assistant may consult the application's own public documentation to ground its responses. It does not access personal data of other people beyond the Volunteer's own context.

### 3.2 Limitations

The assistant's responses:

- May contain errors, omissions, or out-of-date information.
- Do not constitute legal, medical, financial, or any other form of professional advice.
- Do not replace decisions that fall to the Board of Directors, the Coordinators, or any other person with formal responsibilities in the Association.

The Volunteer is responsible for verifying any relevant information before acting on it.

## 4. Data Processing

### 4.1 Data sent to the AI Provider

When the Volunteer interacts with the assistant, the following is sent to the AI Provider:

- The content of the Volunteer's message.
- The Volunteer's display name, preferred language, and the active teams and roles within the Association.
- The page or section of the application from which the assistant was invoked.
- The history of the ongoing conversation.

Passwords, access tokens, banking details, health data, and personal data of other Volunteers beyond what is described above are **not** sent.

### 4.2 Provider and international transfer

The AI Provider currently used is **Anthropic, PBC**, headquartered in the United States. Processing by the AI Provider involves an international transfer outside the European Economic Area, covered by the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission.

The AI Provider acts as a data processor on behalf of the Association. Data sent is not used to train the AI Provider's models.

The Association may change AI Provider in the future and will, in such a case, update these Terms before the change takes effect.

### 4.3 Retention

Conversations are stored in the Association's systems for a configurable retention period. The current period is shown to the Volunteer before any conversation begins. After that period, conversations are permanently deleted.

### 4.4 Visibility

The full log of each conversation is accessible to:

- The Volunteer themselves, from their personal history.
- The Association's administrators, for quality control, abuse prevention, and legal compliance.

### 4.5 Legal basis

Processing is carried out on the basis of the Volunteer's express consent, given by accepting these Terms. Consent may be withdrawn at any time (see section 6).

### 4.6 Volunteer rights

The Volunteer may exercise the rights recognized under the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") and Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 ("LOPD-GDD") — access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction, and portability — by contacting the Board of Directors at **board@nobodies.team**.

Deletion of the conversational history can be requested at any time and does not affect the rest of the relationship between the Volunteer and the Association.

## 5. Acceptable Use

The Volunteer agrees not to use the assistant to:

- Submit confidential information of third parties without proper authorization.
- Generate unlawful, defamatory, discriminatory, or harassing content, or content that infringes intellectual property rights.
- Circumvent access controls, governance processes, or disciplinary procedures established in the Statutes.
- Impersonate another person or misrepresent their membership in the Association.

Misuse of the assistant may result in suspension of access to it and, where applicable, the measures provided for in the Statutes.

## 6. Withdrawal of Consent

The Volunteer can stop using the assistant at any time simply by closing the conversation window. Withdrawal of consent can be formalized by contacting the Board of Directors at **board@nobodies.team**, which will trigger deletion of all conversations associated with the Volunteer.

Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out previously.

## 7. Modifications

The Association may modify these Terms when service conditions, the AI Provider, retention periods, or the applicable legal framework change. Substantial modifications will require the Volunteer to re-accept the Terms before continuing to use the assistant.

## 8. Governing Law

These Terms are governed by Spanish law. Any dispute will first be submitted to the Association's internal resolution procedures set out in the Statutes and, failing that, to the competent courts and tribunals.
