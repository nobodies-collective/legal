# Confidentiality Agreement for Consent and Safer Space Teams

**Asociación Nobodies Collective**
**Event: Elsewhere**

---

\*In the event of any discrepancy between the Spanish version and the English version, the Spanish version shall prevail.

---

## 1. Purpose

This Agreement sets out the confidentiality obligations that apply to anyone involved in consent, Safer Space, wellbeing, report handling, participant support, or incident response activities on behalf of Asociación Nobodies Collective ("the Association") in connection with Elsewhere.

These roles may involve access to sensitive information about participants, witnesses, reporters, affected persons, volunteers, support staff, or coordination teams. The trust of people seeking support depends on that information being handled with care, necessity, and respect.

By accepting this Agreement, the signatory confirms that they have read, understood, and agree to comply with the obligations set out below.

## 2. People Covered by this Agreement

This Agreement applies to anyone who, on site or remotely, is involved in any of the following activities:

- Consent Team
- Safer Space Team
- receiving, triaging, or following up reports or participant communications
- managing reporting boxes, forms, communication channels, or incident documentation
- support, wellbeing, mediation, escalation, or coordination related to consent or safety cases
- any other authorised role that may access confidential information in connection with these activities

The Association may require this Agreement to be signed before granting access to information, tools, or channels related to these roles.

## 3. Definitions

### 3.1 Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any written, oral, visual, electronic, or other information shared in the context of a support request, report, concern, incident, case, investigation, internal review, or response related to consent, safety, wellbeing, or conduct.

It includes, but is not limited to:

- the content of reports, conversations, notes, logs, forms, messages, emails, working documents, or internal decisions
- information that may directly or indirectly identify a reporter, affected person, witness, person of concern, volunteer, or team member
- information about location, schedules, accommodation, contact details, health, identity, relationships, conflicts, support measures, or disciplinary measures
- information shared expressly with an expectation of confidentiality
- information labelled or treated as confidential by the Association or responsible teams

Information that a person has made public themselves is not covered solely because it is public. However, combining public information with non-public information may still be confidential if it identifies a person or reveals a case.

### 3.2 Personal Data

"Personal Data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable person. This may include, among other things, legal name, chosen name, playa or event name, address, telephone number, email address, social media profiles, online identifiers, date of birth, medical or wellbeing information, racial or ethnic origin, religion, sexual orientation, gender identity, relationships, location, or any combination of data that could identify a person.

### 3.3 Participant

"Participant" means anyone who attends, has attended, contributes to, collaborates with, or is connected to Elsewhere or Association activities, including reporters, affected persons, witnesses, volunteers, artists, support staff, team members, directors, suppliers, and other people present or involved.

## 4. Confidentiality Obligations

The signatory agrees to:

1. use Confidential Information only for the authorised purpose for which it was shared;
2. share Confidential Information only with authorised people who need to know it for a legitimate support, safety, wellbeing, coordination, governance, or legal-compliance purpose;
3. not discuss cases, reports, people involved, or identifying details with friends, partners, other participants, unauthorised volunteers, or third parties;
4. not use Confidential Information for personal, social, political, creative, reputational, or other purposes outside the authorised role;
5. treat Confidential Information with particular care during and after the event;
6. ask the responsible person or team for guidance before sharing information where there is any doubt about whether disclosure is permitted;
7. promptly inform the responsible person or team if they believe that unauthorised disclosure, loss of information, improper access, or any other confidentiality incident has occurred.

## 5. Authorised Channels and Minimisation

Confidential Information must be shared only through channels, tools, and procedures approved by the Association or the responsible teams.

The signatory agrees to apply the principle of minimisation: share the least information necessary, with the fewest people necessary, for the shortest time necessary to achieve the legitimate purpose.

Where possible, information should be anonymised or pseudonymised before being shared for coordination, review, training, or organisational learning.

## 6. Exceptions and Escalation

Confidentiality does not prevent action where there is a legitimate and proportionate need to protect a person or comply with a legal obligation.

The signatory may share Confidential Information, only to the extent strictly necessary, in the following cases:

- where there is a clear and imminent risk of death, serious physical harm, violence, abuse, or serious danger to a person;
- where it is necessary to provide immediate support or escalate a situation to the designated responsible people;
- where a person has given explicit consent for a specific disclosure;
- where the Association is legally required to retain, disclose, or report information;
- where the Board of Directors or another designated responsible person needs the information to manage a complaint, conflict, safety measure, disciplinary decision, or compliance matter.

Where it is safe and appropriate, the wishes of the reporter or affected person about how their information is shared will be respected. If emergency services, local authorities, or other external third parties need to be contacted, that decision should be made by the designated responsible people, unless there is an immediate emergency where waiting could increase the risk.

## 7. Information Security

The signatory agrees to take reasonable steps to protect Confidential Information, including:

- not leaving notes, devices, or documents accessible to unauthorised people;
- protecting devices and accounts with appropriate passwords;
- not forwarding, copying, photographing, downloading, or storing Confidential Information outside authorised systems unless necessary and permitted;
- deleting or returning local copies when they are no longer needed or when the Association requests it;
- not using personal tools, shared accounts, or insecure channels to process Confidential Information unless expressly authorised.

## 8. Duration

The confidentiality obligations continue after the end of a shift, role, event, volunteer relationship, or team membership. Confidential Information does not become public or stop being protected because time has passed.

## 9. Relationship with Data Protection

The Association will process Personal Data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679, Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 on Personal Data Protection and Digital Rights, and applicable Association policies.

The signatory acknowledges that they may process Personal Data only on the Association's instructions and only for authorised purposes connected to their role.

## 10. Breach

A breach of this Agreement may cause harm to participants, teams, and the Association. The Association may take proportionate measures, including withdrawal of access, removal from shifts or roles, removal from teams, disciplinary measures under the Statutes, or any other measure necessary to protect people and the Association.

## 11. Confirmation

By signing or accepting this document, the signatory confirms that:

- they have read and understood this Agreement;
- they understand that they are bound to keep covered information confidential;
- they agree to act with care, discretion, and respect toward the people involved;
- they agree to ask for guidance before sharing information if there is any doubt.
