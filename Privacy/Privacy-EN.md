## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") explains how Asociación Nobodies Collective ("the Association") processes the personal data of people who use its membership and volunteer management application ("the application") or who interact with the Association through it.

Processing is carried out in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") and Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 of 5 December on Personal Data Protection and Digital Rights ("LOPD-GDD").

This Policy is informational and complements section 4 (Data Protection and GDPR) of the Volunteer Agreement and the AI Assistant Terms, published at [github.com/nobodies-collective/legal](https://github.com/nobodies-collective/legal).

## 3. Data We Process

The Association processes the following categories of personal data:

- **Identity and account** — Legal name, burner name (the name you are known by in the community), email addresses and, if you sign in with Google, the name and email address Google provides to us. If the Association gives you a @nobodies.team account, that account's details too.
- **Profile** — City, country, bio, pronouns, birthday (day and month only, never the year), profile picture, languages, emergency contact, and optional contact details (phone, Signal, Telegram, WhatsApp, Discord or others).
- **Participation** — Team memberships, assigned roles, volunteer shifts, participation in events and barrios, and tickets.
- **Colaborador and Asociado applications** — The applications submitted, including motivation statements, and the Board's decisions on them. For Asociados, also participation in Assembly votes.
- **Financial data** — For expense reimbursements: legal name, IBAN, expense details and receipts. For Store purchases: name, email address and, when an invoice is requested, tax ID and address.
- **Health and dietary data (optional)** — Medical conditions, dietary preferences, allergies and intolerances, only if you choose to provide them.
- **Communications** — Communication preferences, emails we send you, messages, feedback, survey responses and, if you use the help assistant, your conversations with it.
- **Consent** — A record of the legal documents you have accepted, with the date, IP address and browser user agent at the moment of acceptance.
- **Activity log** — An audit log of actions taken in the application by you, by other members with management roles, or by automated processes that affect you.

## 4. Purposes of Processing

The Association processes personal data to:

- Manage your relationship with the Association and your participation in teams, shifts, events and barrios.
- Handle Colaborador and Asociado applications and maintain the registers of members and collaborators as required by Spanish law.
- Run Assembly votes and record their results.
- Create and manage @nobodies.team accounts and give access to each team's groups and shared drives.
- Manage tickets, Store purchases, invoices and expense reimbursements.
- Communicate Association activities, events and updates, in line with your communication preferences.
- Help members contact each other, according to the visibility you choose for each piece of information.
- Look after safety and logistics at events, such as emergency contacts, medical information and dietary needs.
- Answer your questions through the help assistant, only if you choose to use it.
- Fulfil the Association's legal, accounting, tax and statutory obligations.

The Association does not sell personal data or share it for purposes unrelated to its activities.

## 5. Legal Basis

The Association processes personal data on the following legal bases:

- **Relationship with the Association (GDPR Article 6(1)(b))** — Processing necessary to manage your participation as a Volunteer, Colaborador or Asociado, your tickets and your purchases.
- **Legal obligation (GDPR Article 6(1)(c))** — Keeping the register of members (Organic Law 1/2002) and the accounting and tax records (Commercial Code and Law 58/2003, General Tax Law).
- **Legitimate interest (GDPR Article 6(1)(f))** — Managing the Association's activities, organisational communications, the security of the application and the audit log.
- **Consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a))** — Optional data you choose to provide, non-essential communications and use of the help assistant.
- **Explicit consent (GDPR Article 9(2)(a))** — Health data, which is processed only if you choose to provide it.

You may withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before it.

## 6. Service Providers and International Transfers

### 6.1 Providers

The Association uses the following service providers. Each receives only the data needed for the function stated:

- **Google (sign-in)** — To sign in with your Google account: your name and email address. ([Privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy))
- **Google Workspace** — To create @nobodies.team accounts and manage team groups and shared drives: your name and email address.
- **Gmail (Google Workspace)** — To deliver the emails we send you: your email address, your name and the message content.
- **Google Maps** — For location search and maps: the place you type and your IP address, sent by your browser. ([Privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy))
- **Stripe** — For card payments in the Store: buyer name and email address. Card details are entered directly with Stripe and the Association does not receive them. ([Privacy policy](https://stripe.com/privacy))
- **Holded** — For the Association's accounting. For expense reimbursements: legal name, burner name, IBAN, expense details and receipts. For Store invoices: buyer name, tax ID, address and email address. ([Privacy policy](https://www.holded.com/privacy))
- **Ticket Tailor** — For selling and issuing tickets: ticket holder name and email address, and check-ins. ([Privacy policy](https://www.tickettailor.com/legal/privacy-policy))
- **MailerLite** — For mailing lists: your email address and the mailing groups you belong to (for example, ticket holders or shift volunteers). Not shared if you have opted out of marketing emails. ([Privacy policy](https://www.mailerlite.com/legal/privacy-policy))
- **Anthropic** — For the in-app help assistant, only when you use it: your messages, together with your burner name, language, membership tier, roles, teams and upcoming shifts. The terms of this service are set out in the AI Assistant Terms. ([Privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy))

These providers process data on the Association's behalf and solely for the purposes described in this Policy.

Personal data may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to fulfil the Association's legal obligations.

### 6.2 International Transfers

Some providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where they do, the transfer is covered by appropriate safeguards, such as the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission.

In particular, Anthropic, PBC is headquartered in the United States. Processing by Anthropic involves an international transfer outside the European Economic Area, covered by the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission. Data sent is not used to train its models.

## 7. Data Retention

- **Active account** — Data is kept while your account is active.
- **No expiry for inactivity** — Accounts are not currently deleted for inactivity: your data is kept until you request its deletion.
- **Account deletion** — When you request deletion, your team memberships and roles end immediately. Your personal data is anonymised automatically 30 days after the request or, if you hold a ticket for the current event, when the post-event hold period ends, whichever is later. Until then you can cancel the request.
- **Help assistant conversations** — Kept for the retention period shown to you before you start a conversation, then permanently deleted.

After deletion, the Association keeps only the following, because the law requires it or because it is needed to show compliance with the law:

- The record of who was a member or collaborator, which offices they held and which shifts they covered in each year (Organic Law 1/2002), linked to an anonymised account, without your name or contact details.
- Accounting records and their supporting documents, for six years (Article 30 of the Commercial Code) and four years for tax purposes (Article 66 of Law 58/2003, General Tax Law). Expense reimbursements are kept in full, including legal name and IBAN, because they are accounting vouchers.
- Consent records (including the IP address and user agent at the moment of acceptance) and audit records, which show that processing had a legal basis and that the deletion was carried out.

## 8. Your Rights

You may exercise the following rights at any time:

- **Access** — Request a copy of your personal data. Under **Profile → My Data**, **Download My Data** gives you a file with all the data the application holds about you.
- **Rectification** — Correct inaccurate or incomplete data. You can edit your profile directly in the application.
- **Erasure** — Request deletion of your data. You can request it from **Profile → My Data**, on the terms described in section 7.
- **Portability** — Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format. The **Download My Data** file is in JSON format.
- **Restriction** — Request limitation of processing.
- **Objection** — Object to certain processing. You can choose which emails you receive under **Communication Preferences**.

To exercise any right you cannot manage directly in the application, write to **dpo@nobodies.team** or to the Board of Directors at **board@nobodies.team**.

## 9. Cookies

The application uses only essential cookies, needed to:

- Authenticate you and manage your session.
- Protect forms against forgery.
- Remember your preferred language.
- Remember that you have seen the cookie notice.

The Association does not use analytics, advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

## 10. Security

The Association applies appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including:

- Encryption in transit (HTTPS).
- Secure sign-in with Google or with single-use links sent by email.
- Role-based access controls and audit logging. Access to medical information is logged, and you can see that log on your profile.
- Regular security reviews.

## 11. Changes

The Association may amend this Policy. Amendments require approval by the Board of Directors and are published at [github.com/nobodies-collective/legal](https://github.com/nobodies-collective/legal). Material changes will be communicated by email to active members.

## 12. Contact and Complaints

For any question about this Policy or about how your data is processed, write to **dpo@nobodies.team**.

If you are not satisfied with the response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) at [www.aepd.es](https://www.aepd.es).
