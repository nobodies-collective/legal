# Privacy Policy

**Asociación Nobodies Collective**

\*In the event of any discrepancy between the Spanish version and the English version, the Spanish version shall prevail.

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") explains how Asociación Nobodies Collective ("the Association") processes the personal data of people who use its membership and volunteer management application ("the application") or who interact with the Association through it.

Processing is carried out in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") and Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 of 5 December on Personal Data Protection and Digital Rights ("LOPD-GDD").

This Policy is informational and complements section 4 (Data Protection and GDPR) of the Volunteer Agreement and the AI Assistant Terms, published at [github.com/nobodies-collective/legal](https://github.com/nobodies-collective/legal).

## 2. Data Controller

- **Controller:** Asociación Nobodies Collective, a Spanish association (*asociación*) registered under Spanish Organic Law 1/2002 of 22 March regulating the Right of Association.
- **Tax ID (NIF):** G26726299
- **Registered office:** Glorieta de Bilbao 1, 3º Derecha, 28004, Madrid.
- **Data Protection Officer:** designated under GDPR Article 37; contact at **dpo@nobodies.team**

## 3. Data We Process

The Association processes the following categories of personal data. The examples given are illustrative, not exhaustive:

- **Identity and contact** — Data that identifies you and lets us contact you, such as your legal name, your burner name (the name you are known by in the community), your email addresses and your account details.
- **Profile** — Information you add to your profile, such as your photo, city, languages, emergency contact or additional contact details.
- **Participation and volunteering** — Your activity in the Association, such as teams and roles, shifts, participation in events, and tickets.
- **Membership and governance** — Colaborador or Asociado applications and the decisions on them, offices held, and participation in Assembly votes.
- **Payments and finance** — The data needed for purchases, invoices and expense reimbursements, such as billing details, bank details and receipts.
- **Communications** — Your communication preferences and the messages you exchange with the Association or through the application, such as emails, feedback, survey responses or conversations with the help assistant.
- **Technical and usage data** — Data generated by your use of the application, such as your IP address and browser, the record of legal documents you have accepted, and the audit log of actions that affect you.
- **Health data (optional)** — Health information you choose to provide, such as medical conditions, allergies or dietary needs.

## 4. Purposes of Processing

The Association processes personal data to:

- Manage your relationship with the Association and organise its activities and events, including their safety and logistics.
- Coordinate volunteering, such as teams, roles, shifts and access to the Association's shared tools.
- Manage membership and governance: Colaborador and Asociado applications, the registers of members and collaborators, offices and Assembly votes.
- Manage tickets, purchases, payments, invoices and expense reimbursements.
- Communicate the Association's activities and updates in line with your preferences, and help members contact each other according to the visibility you choose.
- Provide optional features, such as the help assistant, when you choose to use them.
- Keep the application secure and show compliance with the law.
- Fulfil the Association's legal, accounting, tax and statutory obligations.

The Association does not sell personal data or share it for purposes unrelated to its activities.

## 5. Legal Basis

The Association processes personal data on the following legal bases:

- **Relationship with the Association (GDPR Article 6(1)(b))** — Processing necessary to manage your participation as a Volunteer, Colaborador or Asociado and the services you request, such as tickets or purchases.
- **Legal obligation (GDPR Article 6(1)(c))** — Keeping the register of members (Organic Law 1/2002) and the accounting and tax records (Commercial Code and Law 58/2003, General Tax Law).
- **Legitimate interest (GDPR Article 6(1)(f))** — Organising the Association's activities, organisational communications, the security of the application and the audit log.
- **Consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a))** — Optional data you choose to provide, non-essential communications and optional features, such as the help assistant.
- **Explicit consent (GDPR Article 9(2)(a))** — Health data, which is processed only if you choose to provide it.

You may withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before it.

## 6. Service Providers and International Transfers

### 6.1 Providers

The Association uses service providers for the functions below. Each provider receives only the data needed for its function. The names given are the providers currently in use:

- **Sign-in and workspace** — Authentication, @nobodies.team accounts and teams' shared tools: identity and contact data. Currently Google Workspace.
- **Email delivery** — Delivering the emails we send you: your email address and the message content. Currently Gmail (Google Workspace).
- **Maps** — Location search and maps: the locations you look up and the technical data your browser sends. Currently Google Maps.
- **Payment processing** — Card payments: purchase and buyer details. Card details are entered directly with the provider and the Association does not receive them. Currently Stripe.
- **Accounting** — Accounting, invoicing and expense reimbursements: the financial data needed for each transaction. Currently Holded.
- **Ticketing** — Selling, issuing and checking tickets: ticket holders' identity and contact data. Currently Ticket Tailor.
- **Mailing lists** — Mailing list sends, in line with your communication preferences: your email address and the lists you belong to. Currently MailerLite.
- **Help assistant** — Answering your questions, only when you use the assistant: your messages and the context needed to answer them, as detailed in the AI Assistant Terms. Currently Anthropic.

These providers process data on the Association's behalf and solely for the purposes described in this Policy. The Association may replace them or use other providers for functions of the same nature, with equivalent safeguards; the updated list is published as described in section 11.

Personal data may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to fulfil the Association's legal obligations.

### 6.2 International Transfers

Some providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where they do, the transfer is covered by a European Commission adequacy decision or by appropriate safeguards, such as the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the Commission.

In particular, the help assistant's current provider, Anthropic, PBC, is headquartered in the United States; using the assistant involves an international transfer covered by the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission. Data sent is not used to train its models.

## 7. Data Retention

- **Active account** — Data is kept while your account is active. Accounts are not currently deleted for inactivity: your data is kept until you request its deletion.
- **Account deletion** — When you request deletion, your personal data is erased or anonymised within a reasonable period after your request, subject to the legal retention obligations set out below.
- **Help assistant conversations** — Kept for the period set out in the AI Assistant Terms, then permanently deleted.

After deletion, the Association keeps only the following, because the law requires it or because it is needed to show compliance with the law:

- The register of members and collaborators, with the offices held and participation in each year (Organic Law 1/2002), unlinked from your name and contact details.
- Accounting records and their supporting documents, including expense reimbursements and the personal data they contain, for the periods required by law (six years under Article 30 of the Commercial Code and four years for tax purposes under Article 66 of Law 58/2003, General Tax Law).
- Consent and audit records, which show that processing had a legal basis and that the deletion was carried out.

## 8. Your Rights

You may exercise the following rights at any time:

- **Access** — Request a copy of your personal data.
- **Rectification** — Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Erasure** — Request deletion of your data, on the terms described in section 7.
- **Portability** — Receive your data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format.
- **Restriction** — Request limitation of processing.
- **Objection** — Object to certain processing, including non-essential communications.

The application gives you tools to download your data, update your profile, manage your communication preferences and request deletion of your account. To exercise any right you cannot manage directly in the application, write to **dpo@nobodies.team** or to the Board of Directors at **board@nobodies.team**.

## 9. Cookies

The application uses only essential cookies, needed to:

- Authenticate you and manage your session.
- Protect forms against forgery.
- Remember your preferred language.
- Remember that you have seen the cookie notice.

The Association does not use analytics, advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

## 10. Security

The Association applies appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including:

- Encryption in transit (HTTPS).
- Secure sign-in methods.
- Role-based access controls and audit logging, including logging of access to health data.
- Regular security reviews.

## 11. Changes

The Association may amend this Policy, including the list of providers in section 6, to reflect changes in its activities, the application or the law. Amendments require approval by the Board of Directors and are published at [github.com/nobodies-collective/legal](https://github.com/nobodies-collective/legal). Material changes will be communicated by email to active members.

## 12. Contact and Complaints

For any question about this Policy or about how your data is processed, write to **dpo@nobodies.team**.

If you are not satisfied with the response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) at [www.aepd.es](https://www.aepd.es).
